# User Feedback Form

**Version:** v1
**Purpose:** Collect structured feedback from beta users on usability, data gaps, bugs, and suggestions.

**Live form:** https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe4hQcIMxplSJR44xBjhGSWRCcukj8hlWhWNH8WxJS8C5xj9g/viewform

The form is live and linked from the project website and the README. The questions and setup notes below document its contents.

**Form title:** Global Digital Infrastructure Political Economy Observatory: User Feedback

**Description:** Thank you for trying the Global Digital Infrastructure Political Economy Observatory. This short form helps us understand what is working, what is missing, and what to improve. Every question is optional except the first.

## Questions

**1. How did you use the platform?** (Multiple choice, required)
Research · Journalism · Policy analysis · Industry or commercial · Teaching or learning · Just exploring · Other

**2. What were you trying to find out?** (Paragraph, optional)
Helps us understand real use cases rather than assumed ones.

**3. Were you able to find it?** (Multiple choice, optional)
Yes, easily · Yes, but it took some effort · Partly · No

**4. How easy was the platform to use?** (Linear scale 1 to 5, optional)
1 = Difficult, 5 = Very easy

**5. Which parts were confusing or hard to use?** (Paragraph, optional)

**6. Was any data missing, out of date, or incorrect?** (Paragraph, optional)
Please name the specific cable, country, or policy event if you can, and include a source if you have one.

**7. Did you encounter any bugs or errors?** (Paragraph, optional)
What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

**8. What would make this more useful to you?** (Paragraph, optional)
Additional data, indicators, export formats, or features.

**9. How likely are you to use the platform again?** (Linear scale 1 to 5, optional)
1 = Not likely, 5 = Very likely

**10. May we contact you about your feedback?** (Short answer, optional)
Leave your email if you are open to a follow-up conversation.

## Setup notes

- Link a response spreadsheet from the Responses tab so submissions collect in one place.
